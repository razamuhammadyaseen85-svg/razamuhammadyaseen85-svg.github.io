Privacy Policy – Job Bot

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Job Bot is a personal tool used only by its owner, Muhammad Yaseen Raza. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

  • Google Sheets: reads and adds rows to one job tracker spreadsheet owned by me.
  • Gmail: sends a daily job summary email to myself, and may read job alert emails sent to my own inbox.

How the data is used

The data is used only to track my own job applications. It is not sold, shared or transferred to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.

Storage

Data stays in my own Google account and on my own computer, where the automation runs.

Google API Services

Job Bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
